Who it is for

Lawyers & Legal Teams

Sanitize pleadings, client notices, FIR extracts, witness statements, and arbitration briefs—stripping Aadhaar, PAN, EPIC, vehicle numbers, and client names before drafting summaries or arguments with AI.

Doctors & Clinics

De-identify discharge summaries, lab reports, ABHA 14-digit health IDs, UHID/MRN numbers, phone numbers, and patient honorifics before checking differential notes or patient instructions.

HR & Chartered Accountants

Mask GSTIN, PAN, IFSC, bank account numbers, UPI handles, salary annexures, and employee IDs before using LLMs to structure tax memos, compliance checklists, or HR policies.

Developers & SREs

Scrub JWTs, API keys, signed URLs with query tokens, IPv4/IPv6 addresses, and customer emails from production stack traces and JSON payloads before debugging with an AI assistant.

How it works

1

Detect & Validate Locally

Paste text or drop a file. MaskPrompt runs regex patterns paired with mathematical checksums (Verhoeff for Aadhaar, Luhn for cards, mod-36 for GSTIN) entirely inside browser memory.

2

Consistent Token Substitution

Identical values always receive the same placeholder (such as [AADHAAR_1] or [PERSON_1]), preserving relational context so ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini can reason accurately.

3

One-Click Restoration

Paste the AI reply into Step 2 to re-hydrate the original names and IDs. Need to close your tab? Export the mapping encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a passphrase you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text or mapping uploaded to any server?

No. MaskPrompt is a single self-contained HTML file with zero external scripts, zero analytics, and zero network calls. All detection, diffing, and AES-GCM encryption execute locally in your browser's RAM and vanish when you close or refresh the tab unless you explicitly download an encrypted mapping file.

Does using MaskPrompt make AI usage compliant with India's DPDP Act?

It significantly reduces data exposure risk by stripping direct identifiers before data leaves your device, supporting data minimization practices under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. However, technical redaction alone does not constitute legal compliance or legal advice—you must still verify contractual data-processing terms, consent obligations, and indirect re-identification risks with your legal counsel.

How does the Restore step work?

When MaskPrompt masks your text in Step 1, it keeps an in-memory dictionary mapping each generated placeholder (for example, [PAN_1]) to its exact original string. In Step 2, MaskPrompt scans the AI's response for those placeholder tokens and replaces them with your original values locally.

What can MaskPrompt not detect automatically?

Unlabelled free-form names without honorifics (e.g., "Rohan met Priya at the cafe"), rare contextual facts ("the only cardiologist in Ward 4"), or scanned text inside images cannot be reliably caught by deterministic rules. Always add known client, patient, or company names to the Custom Terms box and inspect the Interactive Preview before copying.

Can I use MaskPrompt completely offline?

Yes. Save this page (File → Save Page As) or download index.html from the GitHub repository and open it directly in any modern browser on an air-gapped or offline machine. Every feature, including checksum validation and WebCrypto encryption, works without an internet connection.

Is MaskPrompt free and open source?

Yes. MaskPrompt is 100% free and open source under the MIT License, created by Shrijeet Singh. You can inspect the entire source code in a single file, audit every regular expression, or self-host it on your firm's internal intranet.
